The Grey Wolf

Type: Canine (Canis lupus)

Habitat: across the Northern Hemisphere in North America, Europe, and Asia

Diet: Carnivorous (large, hooved animals (ungulates), supplemented by smaller prey, plants, and scavenged food)

Lifespan: 6-8 years in the wild

Fun fact: Wolf pups open their eyes with a blue hue that typically changes color around 6 to 8 weeks of age.
